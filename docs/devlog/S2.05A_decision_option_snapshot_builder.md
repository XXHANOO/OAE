## 6. Implementation Details

`DecisionOptionSnapshot` is a frozen slots dataclass with exactly the required
contract, canonical decision timestamp, selected quote, exact age, and quality
fields. `build_decision_option_snapshot(contract, quotes, decision_ts)` first
uses `to_utc`, validates every quote—future rows included—using exact
`source` and `contract_id` equality, then delegates temporal selection to
`select_asof_quote`.

For an empty or all-future stream it returns `MISSING` with no synthetic quote
and no classifier call. Otherwise it keeps the exact selected DTO, calls the
approved `quote_age_seconds`, and calls `classify_quote_quality` with the
frozen `2.0` maximum age. No exception is caught: canonical timezone failures
and `AsOfSelectionIntegrityError` propagate unchanged.

changed: NO. Quote-quality classifier changed: NO. Contract DTO changed: NO.
Quote DTO changed: NO. Temporal selection before quality: YES. Latest invalid
quote may be selected: YES, intentionally. MISSING emitted by selector: NO.
MISSING emitted by snapshot layer when no eligible quote: YES. Exact selected
quote DTO and exact quote age retained: YES. The 2.000-second validity
boundary remains inclusive.

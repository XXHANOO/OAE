## 6. Implementation Details

## Timestamp / DST Contract

- Persistent timestamp standard: UTC / timezone-aware
- Market timezone: America/New_York / `ZoneInfo`
- Naive datetime policy: REJECT
- Fixed UTC offset used: NO
- Frozen decision: 13:30 ET
- Winter decision UTC: 18:30
- Summer decision UTC: 17:30
- Session open: 09:30 ET
- PM x11 reference: 16:00 ET
- Half-open interval: `[start,end)`
- As-of predicate: `event_ts <= asof_ts`
- Bar availability: `bar_end_ts <= asof_ts`
- Future timestamp considered fresh: NO
- 2.000s fresh: YES
- 2.001s fresh: NO

The module defines exactly the required public functions:

```text
to_utc
to_market_time
session_date_et_from_timestamp
session_open_ts_et
session_open_ts_utc
decision_ts_et
decision_ts_utc
pm_reference_ts_et
pm_reference_ts_utc
is_available_asof
age_seconds
is_fresh_asof
in_half_open_interval
bar_is_usable_asof
```

One private validator rejects a timestamp when `tzinfo` is absent or
`utcoffset()` returns `None`, always with a `timezone-aware` error message.
The only local timestamp constructors are the frozen safe 09:30, 13:30, and
16:00 market clocks. No generic arbitrary-local-time localization helper was
introduced. The implementation uses only Python standard-library `datetime`,
`zoneinfo`, and `math`.

## DST Arithmetic Evidence

- Spring-forward elapsed-time test: PASS
- Expected and observed: 2026-03-08 01:30 ET to 03:30 ET equals 3600 seconds
  of actual elapsed time, not 7200.
- Fall-back elapsed-time test: PASS
- Expected and observed: 2026-11-01 00:30 ET to 02:30 ET equals 10800 seconds
  of actual elapsed time, not 7200.

Elapsed arithmetic normalizes both operands to the UTC timeline before
subtraction. As-of, half-open interval, and bar comparisons likewise normalize
to UTC instants before comparison.

- 1.999s: FRESH
- 2.000s: FRESH
- 2.001s: STALE TEMPORALLY
- Future quote/event: NOT FRESH

## Security / Leakage Foundation

- Future timestamps are not available as-of.
- Future bars are not usable as-of.
- Naive datetimes cannot enter temporal comparison primitives.
- No nearest-timestamp semantic was introduced.
- No system-local timezone semantic was introduced.
- No fixed UTC-5 or UTC-4 market-timezone semantic was introduced.

These utilities are foundations for future leakage assertions but do not
implement the leakage engine in S1.11.

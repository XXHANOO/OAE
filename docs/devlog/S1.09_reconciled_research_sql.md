### PIT / Evidence Boundary

Original point-in-time forecasts are persisted separately from later realized
outcomes. `forecast_evaluations` joins later `realized_y` to the original
forecast identity and does not retrospectively rewrite `forecast_runs` or
duplicate its Q10/Q50/Q90 values.

Neighbor intermediate similarity, recency, combined-raw, and normalized
weights are all preserved. Candidate leg bid/ask values and quote timestamps
are persisted as nullable evidence when available. Invalid/incomplete
candidates may persist with `candidate_valid = false` without fabricated
quotes or economics.

NO TRADE decisions may persist without an artificial forecast or candidate.
Unsettled trades may exist before official settlement with nullable realized
settlement, payoff, P&L, return, and win fields. No future information is
required by the DDL.

No foreign key, standalone UNIQUE constraint, DEFAULT clause, standalone
index, model/trading threshold CHECK, or invented `forecast_error` semantic
was added.

## 6. Implementation Details

`OptionContractRecord` inherits the existing strict, frozen `StrictModel` and
declares exactly these fields in canonical persistence order: `contract_id`,
`vendor_symbol`, `product`, `root_symbol`, `underlying`, `option_type`,
`strike`, `expiration_date`, `settlement_style`, `exercise_style`,
`multiplier`, `series_type`, `expiration_ts_utc`, `first_seen_date`,
`last_seen_date`, and `source`.

`contract_id` is required, non-empty, preserved exactly, and otherwise opaque.
No UUID, OCC, hashing, concatenation, or other generation algorithm was added.
The other required identity strings and `source` are non-empty and preserved
without trimming, case conversion, alias mapping, or inference. This permits
the canonical `product=SPX`, `underlying=SPX`, `root_symbol=SPXW` distinction
and structurally represents XSP contracts without selecting either product.

`option_type`, `settlement_style`, and `exercise_style` reuse the existing
canonical enums. Both AM and PM settlement and all current exercise styles
remain representable. `strike` remains an unquantized `Decimal`.
`multiplier` uses a strict Pydantic integer field with `gt=0`, rejecting zero,
negative values, booleans, floats, and strings without hard-coding 100.
`series_type` remains nullable and otherwise preserves arbitrary text,
including an empty string.

The existing `oae.temporal.to_utc` utility rejects naive expiration
timestamps and converts aware timestamps to equivalent UTC instants. The DTO
does not derive that timestamp from `expiration_date` or assume a provider
expiry clock. Expiration and first/last-seen dates remain explicit and are not
derived or cross-validated. Full canonical review found no explicit
first-seen/last-seen ordering invariant, so the DTO deliberately introduces
none and its contract test confirms structural representability of reversed
dates. No 0DTE, same-day, PM-only, strike, chain, or product eligibility logic
is present.

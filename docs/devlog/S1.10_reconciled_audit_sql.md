`audit.backtest_metrics` includes every Data/Backtest Specification section 86
field plus the reconciled confidence-interval, aggregate out-of-sample EV, and
acceptance-result fields. Its sole primary key is `run_id`; every column is
`NOT NULL`. A failed run can therefore exist without a metrics row, but every
persisted metrics row must contain the complete evidence set.

The acceptance-result check admits exactly `POTENTIAL_ALPHA` and
`NO_VALIDATED_ALPHA`. `n_sessions` retains its formal out-of-sample session
meaning from the frozen specification; the migration does not redefine or
compute it.

### Metric Numeric Policy

The monetary/accounting fields `gross_pnl`, `net_pnl`, `max_drawdown`,
`largest_win`, and `largest_loss` use `DECIMAL(18,6)`. Counts use `BIGINT`:
`n_sessions`, `n_trade_signals`, `n_no_trade`, `call_spread_count`, and
`put_spread_count`. Statistical, rate, calibration, confidence-bound, and EV
fields use `DOUBLE`, including `mean_net_pnl_ci_lower_95` and
`aggregate_oos_ev`.

- No mathematical formula changed: YES
- Formula changes: NO

### Acceptance Boundary

The audit DDL persists acceptance evidence and the resulting historical
classification. The audit DDL does not compute acceptance. No DDL CHECK
implements any of the following:

- 500-session threshold
- 150-signal threshold
- Positive mean NetPnL
- Positive bootstrap confidence-interval lower bound
- Profit factor greater than or equal to 1.20
- Positive aggregate out-of-sample EV

`POTENTIAL_ALPHA` remains a historical acceptance label and is not proof of
future profitability, a live-trading authorization, or a new semantic rule.

### Constraint Boundary

The migration contains exactly two primary keys and two business-domain
checks. It contains no foreign key, default, secondary index, standalone
unique constraint, lifecycle constraint, or threshold constraint. Dataset
snapshot identity remains the `data_snapshot_sha256` provenance column rather
than a new relational table.
